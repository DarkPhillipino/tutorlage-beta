## 5. Do you have to give it?

- **Everyone**: giving us information is voluntary. Without your name, email, date of birth and role we cannot create an account.
- **Payments**: without a card we cannot book a session.
- **Tutors**: without identity, eligibility, bank and screening details, we cannot approve you to tutor.
- **Law**: no law requires you to give us information to use Tutorlage. The law does require us to keep some records once you have (§14).

## 6. Learners under 18

- We process a learner under 18's information only with the **prior consent of their parent or legal guardian** (a "competent person"), who holds the account.
- When a parent or guardian adds a learner, in a separate step from accepting the Terms, we ask them to confirm:
  - that they are the learner's parent or legal guardian;
  - the learner's name, date of birth, school and grade;
  - that they consent to Tutorlage processing that information to match the learner with tutors, run sessions, keep session records and handle payments.
- We record who gave consent, for which learner, what they were shown, and when.
- A parent or guardian can review the learner's information and **withdraw consent at any time**. Withdrawing ends the learner's use of the platform.
- We do not ask learners for more than the service needs, and we never use a learner's information for marketing.

**School results (optional).** We invite a parent or guardian to give us a learner's report card, or, if they do not have one, the marks of each test, assignment and exam from the time the learner had sessions with a tutor. It is optional: booking and sessions work the same without it.
- **Why**: to show the parent the learner's progress, and to measure whether tutoring helps. A tutor's level (and so their price) partly depends on their learners' progress (§10).
- **Consent**: we ask for the parent or guardian's consent, separately, before the first upload. They can withdraw it and ask us to delete the results at any time.
- **Only what is needed**: we use only the subjects the learner is tutored in. If a report card shows other subjects, we do not record those marks.
- **Who sees what**: the parent or guardian and the learner see everything they gave us. A tutor sees **only the change** in the learner's mark for the subject they taught, never the report card itself or other subjects. Tutorlage's staff see the uploaded documents only to check that the marks entered match them.
- **Checked before it counts**: marks count towards a tutor's level only after Tutorlage has checked them against the document.

## 7. Tutor screening (special personal information)

- Every tutor gives us a **photo of their ID or passport**, a **police clearance certificate** issued in the last 6 months, and **proof of their school results or of their experience** (a beginner gives results; an experienced tutor or teacher gives experience). A tutor registered with **SACE** (the South African Council for Educators) **may** also give us their SACE certificate; it is optional, and nobody is refused for not having one. We ask for a new police clearance **every 6 months**.
- We check each document before the tutor may accept requests. If a clearance expires, the tutor cannot accept new requests until they give us a current one.
- A police clearance is information about criminal behaviour, which POPIA treats as **special personal information**. We process it with the tutor's consent. **If you do not agree, you cannot tutor on Tutorlage.** Only the staff who check documents can see them, and we record each time one is opened.
- **Why we keep your ID.** So that every tutor is a real person we can identify. If a crime is reported to have been committed through Tutorlage and we have reason to believe it, we may give the police your tutor profile and ID details together with the evidence. Our safeguarding lead decides, writes down the reason, and shares only what the police need. Where the law requires us to report (for example a sexual offence against a child), we report to the police whatever your agreement says.
- **How long.** We keep your ID copy and police certificate while you tutor and for 3 years after. A document we have verified cannot be deleted or swapped by you while we are entitled to keep it. If you ask us to delete it and we refuse, we will tell you why in writing.

## 8. Who we share it with

- **The person you are matched with**, limited to what the session needs:
  - tutors and learners see each other's names, and the subject, level and time;
  - for a session a parent or guardian booked, **the tutor sees the parent or guardian's name and phone number**, so the parent can be reached during the session;
  - a tutor's name and level are visible to signed-in users who are matched with them.
- **Other people in a video room.** In Tutorlage's video room the others in the room see your name, and your camera and microphone are carried live to them. We do not record or keep the audio or video. A tutor who opens a room for adults lets people in from a waiting area, and sees the names of the people waiting.

## 10. Automated decisions about tutors

- A tutor's level, and so their prices and earnings, is **worked out automatically** from their session statistics:
  - hours taught;
  - rating;
  - repeat bookings;
  - reviews;
  - learner progress, where measured: the change in learners' school marks for the subject, from the report cards and marks parents (or adult learners) give us, counted only after Tutorlage has checked them.
- Tutors can see the criteria for the next level, and their progress towards it, in the app.
- Tutors may ask us how their level was worked out, and ask a person to review it, at support@tutorlage.com.
- Matching a request to tutors (by subject, grade and level) is also automatic, but it does not decide anything about the learner beyond who can accept.

## 11. Cookies and browser storage

- **No advertising or analytics cookies.** Our site does not use them, and does not track you across other websites.
- **What we keep in your browser's storage**:
  - your sign-in session, so you stay signed in;
  - your language choice;
  - while you sign up with Google, the role you chose.
- **Other services' pages.** When you use Google sign-in or Paystack's payment pages, those services set their own cookies under their own policies.
- **Clearing storage.** You can clear your browser's storage at any time. You will then be signed out.

## 12. Marketing

- We do not send marketing messages.
- We send only messages about your account and bookings.
- If we ever send marketing, every message will let you unsubscribe, and we will stop when you ask. We will ask permission first where the law requires it.
- We never send marketing to learners under 18.

## 13. Security, and if something goes wrong

**How we protect your information**:
- access rules on every table of our database, so each person sees only what they are allowed to;
- encrypted connections;
- an admin app that is not on the public internet;
- an audit log of admin actions.

**Payments**:
- card details are entered on Paystack's pages, not ours;
- we never store full card numbers, or tutors' ID numbers or full bank account numbers.

**If there is a security compromise**: if your information may have been accessed by someone unauthorised, we will notify the Information Regulator and you **as soon as reasonably possible after we discover it**. We will tell you what happened, what it could mean for you, what we have done about it, what you can do to protect yourself, and who accessed it if we know.

## 14. How long we keep it

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Account details | While your account is open, then deleted within 90 days of closing, except what is below |
| Bookings, payments, refunds and the session records behind them | **7 years** after the end of the financial year they fall in, because they are part of the company's accounting records |
| Acceptance and consent records | While the account is open plus 5 years, as evidence of what was agreed |

| Tutors' ID copies, police clearances, proof of results or experience, and SACE certificates (if given) | While the person tutors on Tutorlage plus 3 years. Longer if a safety report or an investigation involves them, until it is closed and the time for a claim has passed |
| Tier 1 training and test records | While the person tutors on Tutorlage (or 2 years after their last attempt, if they do not qualify). Answers are then kept only anonymously, to check the test is fair |
| Video room records (who joined which room and when) | Deleted 30 days after the room closes. No audio or video is ever kept |
| Problem reports and safety reports | 5 years, or longer where a report concerns a learner under 18 and the law or an investigation requires it |
| Support emails | 2 years |
| "Tell me when a tutor is available" requests | Not acted on after 60 days; deleted 90 days after you were told or after you asked, whichever applies; you can remove one at any time |
| Choices about tutors (kept, tried someone else) and a tutor's decision not to carry on | While the learner's or tutor's account is open, then deleted with it. Removed by the learner at any time ("Undo") |
| Technical logs | For as long as each provider keeps them under its own policy |
| **Data from the closed test** (accounts and sessions made before 28 October 2026) | **Deleted by 27 November 2026**, except anonymised feedback and counts |

When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it so it cannot identify you.

## 15. Your rights, and how to use them

You may:
- **ask whether we hold information about you**, and for a copy of it and a list of who we have shared it with;
- **ask us to correct or delete** information that is wrong, incomplete, out of date, excessive, or obtained unlawfully, or that we are no longer allowed to keep;
- **object** to our using your information on the basis of legitimate interest (§4);
- **withdraw consent** where we rely on it (it does not affect what we did before);
- **ask for a person to review** an automated decision about you (§10);
- **complain to the Information Regulator** (§16).

**How to ask**:
- **Email our Information Officer** at support@tutorlage.com. You can also use the Information Regulator's prescribed forms for correction or deletion, for an objection to processing, and for records under the Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA), which are on its website, inforegulator.org.za.
- **Identity.** We will ask you to confirm your identity first.
- **When we will reply.** Within a reasonable time, and within 30 days.
