- Do not keep it longer than you need it, do not share it, and do not use it to contact anyone outside the rules in Part A §11.
- Tell us at once at safety@tutorlage.com if it is lost or misused.

## B8. Screening

- Before you can accept requests, you give Tutorlage a **police clearance certificate** that was issued in the last 10 months **when you register**. If you are registered with SACE, you **may** also give us your **SACE certificate**; you do not have to.
- You also give us a **photo of your ID or passport**, and **proof of your school results (if you are a beginner) or of your experience (if you are experienced)**.
- You consent to Tutorlage checking these documents and keeping them as our Privacy Policy (§7) says. **If you do not agree, you cannot tutor on Tutorlage.**
- Tell us at once if anything changes that a new clearance would show.

## B9. Conduct

You agree to:
- the rules in Part A §11, including "Acts we will not allow" (they cover your profile text, photo and banner too);
- the Tutorlage code of conduct;
- being honest about what you do not know. Never present a guess as certain;
- **helping the learner learn, not doing their assessed work.** Do not write, complete or answer homework, assignments, projects, tests or exams that will be marked, even if you are asked to. Explain, use similar examples, and check the learner's own attempt.

## B10. Your teaching materials

- Materials you create stay yours.
- Only share materials you have the right to share.

## B11. Ending the relationship

- You may stop tutoring at any time. Finish or cancel your booked sessions properly when you do.
